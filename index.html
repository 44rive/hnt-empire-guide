The operating model

One empire.
One system of truth.

The Founder dispatches outcomes and interventions—not endless low-level tickets. Departments decompose authorized work, execute inside explicit boundaries, and return evidence, blockers, reviews, and measurable effects.

The browser, Empire CLI, Telegram surface, and department runtimes all consume the same contracts. They are different expressions of the same operating plane, not competing databases or parallel versions of reality.

Founder workflow

From command
to measured effect.

The system is designed to reduce coordination load while keeping consequential action visible, attributable, and interruptible.

  1. 01
    SEE

    Open Now

    Read today’s command, attention, source freshness, blockers, and runtime health.

  2. 02
    DIRECT

    Dispatch an outcome

    Name the desired effect, owner, boundaries, urgency, and acceptance evidence.

  3. 03
    GOVERN

    Authorize execution

    Coord validates state; Law verifies actor, action, resource, and invariant policy.

  4. 04
    EXECUTE

    Let departments work

    Mastra decomposes bounded work and invokes approved services, tools, and workers.

  5. 05
    JUDGE

    Inspect evidence

    Review dossiers, delivery receipts, incidents, source lineage, and measured effect.

What it enables

Operate the whole company without losing the thread.

HNT Empire is being completed as a production operating system—not a polished mockup. As the remaining live gates close, it will give the Founder one place to direct the company and one evidence trail from intention to outcome.

  • 01See today’s command and what needs intervention.
  • 02Dispatch outcomes across one or several departments.
  • 03Trace every task to an authoritative reason and owner.
  • 04Open dossiers for work, evidence, decisions, incidents, and findings.
  • 05Separate healthy infrastructure from actual mission effectiveness.
  • 06Control businesses, bets, obligations, and capital at the holding layer.
  • 07Use the same language and truth from browser, terminal, and Telegram.
  • 08Deploy, detect drift, roll back, restore, and prove the exact running revision.
